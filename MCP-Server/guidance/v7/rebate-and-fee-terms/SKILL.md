# Rebate and fee terms (plain words)

This is a general glossary for reading the contract. Ingenus's own definitions override it.

- **Rebate:** money the supplier gives back to the customer, usually a percent of the customer's net sales of a product. One product, one rate.
- **Administrative fee (admin fee):** a percent of total net sales that the supplier pays or credits, often to a wholesaler, for handling. One rate for the whole customer, not per product.
- **Net sales:** gross invoiced sales less returns and credits, as the contract defines it. The tools read net sales from SAP.
- **Effective date:** the date a rate starts. It may be before the signing date (back-dated).
- **Schedule or exhibit:** an attachment where the rates are listed. Often the most important page.
- **Volume incentive, price protection credit:** other names a contract may use for a rebate-like payment. If a contract uses an unusual name, say which words you treated as a rebate and why.
- **Accrual:** the amount a company sets aside for a rebate or fee it expects to pay. The check compares the contract with what SAP is set up to accrue.
- **Indicative exposure:** a rough figure for the gap over the months since the new rate started. It is not an accounting number. Finance owns the accrual figure.

Not in scope for this check: chargebacks, returns accruals and Medicaid. Do not guess about them.
